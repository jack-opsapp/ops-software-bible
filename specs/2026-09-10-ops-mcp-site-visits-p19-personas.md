Source: [30 Target Customer Character Personas](../research/30%20Target%20Customer%20Character%20Personas.md). These scenarios translate the existing personas' trades, crew sizes and operating needs into acceptance cases. They are designed examples, not quotations, interviews, live test accounts, or thirty completed native-host trials. Never use the separate PERSONA TEST POOL for mutations.

Exact final counts, commits, hashes and phone regression outcomes belong to the phase handoff. This matrix does not expand those proofs.
